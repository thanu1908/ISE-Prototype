⚡ The Problem

Learning Life Skills Shouldn't Depend on a Perfect Environment.

Children with ASD and ID face unique challenges when learning everyday skills in unpredictable real-world settings.

🌀

Unpredictable Real-World Situations

Everyday environments can be overwhelming or difficult to control, making consistent learning difficult.

🔒

Limited Safe Practice

Children may not always have the opportunity to repeatedly practice the same situation in real life.

📐

One-Size-Fits-All Learning

Different learners require different levels of guidance, but traditional approaches don't adapt.

ISE Response

Controlled → Repeatable → Personalized → Supportive
